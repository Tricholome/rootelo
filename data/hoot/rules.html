The Tabletop Community

Hoot rankings track competitive play on Tabletop Simulator, aggregating results from various events organized on the Root Tabletop Tournaments channel. Ratings and match history carry over indefinitely without seasonal resets, providing an uninterrupted record of performance over time.

The Rating Volatility

Controlled by the K-Factor, rating volatility starts at K = 50 for new entrants and smoothly decays toward a veteran floor of K = 25 as games accumulate.

The Price of Inactivity

To keep ratings accurate for returning players, an absence longer than 90 days temporarily increases rating volatility. Controlled by an inactivity multiplier, this allows players returning from a break to adjust faster to their current skill level upon re-entering competition.
