Root Digital League (RDL)

The Root Digital League is an established competitive community organizing structured seasons played via the official digital app. Matches are conducted continuously throughout the season to determine placement on the seasonal ladder.

The Seasonal Lifecycle

While Elo ratings persist across seasons, seasonal match counts reset at the start of each new iteration. Players complete a 10-game calibration phase to establish their seasonal rank. At the conclusion of the season, a global pool rebalancing injects a Loyalty Bonus to all active players to prevent rating deflation.

The Championship Finale

When a season closes, the top four qualified players on the leaderboard advance to a single, all-random final match to crown the Seasonal Champion.
