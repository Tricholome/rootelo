Understanding the fundamental rules and mechanics of Rootelo.

Overview League Simulator

The Digital League

Known for its informal yet highly competitive spirit, the Root Digital League organizes established seasons where every match is played to the bitterest end. You can join the adventure at any time: visit their official site to subscribe, follow the community rules, and start competing for seasonal awards.

The Rating Volatility

Driven by the K-Factor, rating volatility starts high during initial placement before smoothly decaying toward a stable floor as matches accumulate:

Starting No games K = 75 Initiation for previously unranked players.
Returning No games K = 50 Soft-reset for previous season ranked players.
Calibrating 10 games K = 50 High mobility while establishing your rank.
Active 20 games K = 31 Steady decay as match count grows.
Stabilizing 30 games K = 24 Fine-tuning near skill equilibrium.
Veterans 40+ games K = 20 Minimum floor for long-term rank stability.

The Seasonal Lifecycle

While Elo scores are persistent, seasonal game counts are reset. To reclaim their Tier, every player must complete the calibration phase at the start of each season, where restored volatility allows for rapid movement. At a season’s end, a Loyalty Bonus is equally distributed to all players with at least one recorded match. By rebalancing the global pool, this system combats deflation and can even secure a qualification at the finish line.

The Championship Finale

Once the leaderboard is frozen, the top four qualified players advance to a single, all-random match to crown the League Champion. Played either live or asynchronously at the participants' discretion, this final clash provides a definitive conclusion to the current season.
