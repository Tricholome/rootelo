The Homelands Ecosystem

Homelands represent dynamic seasonal clusters formed through recurring matches. Rather than fixed regional factions, they identify stable communities of competitors who consistently share tables and anchor the Woodland's competitive life.

The Frog Tier • Homeland Heralds

Unlike competitive tiers defined strictly by Elo rating, the Frog tier identifies structural pillars within the player network. A Herald maintains the frequent table encounters and matchmaking volume around which a seasonal homeland naturally crystallizes.

Allegiance & Standing

Every match shifts a player's affinity within the network:

  • Partisan — Core competitors maintaining a primary affinity of 65% or higher with their homeland.
  • Squire — Regular participants with an intermediate affinity between 50% and 64%.
  • Friend — Occasional table companions holding less than 50% affinity.
  • Unaligned — Independent competitors not yet tied to a persistent seasonal homeland.
