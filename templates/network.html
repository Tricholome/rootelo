HOMELANDS MAP

The Woodland Cartography

While the Leaderboard tracks competitive mastery through Elo ratings, Homelands maps the social fabric of the Woodland. Rather than asking who defeats whom, this cartography reveals who shares the table with whom—uncovering the natural playgroups and recurring fellowships that bring each season to life.

The Homelands View • Clearings & Chronicles

On the macro map, each emergent community appears as a Clearing rallied around an iconic woodland item. Use the bottom date controls to travel day by day through the season and watch domains rise, expand, or fade away. Clicking any clearing opens its internal constellation:

Inside a clearing, a player's Weight (volume of recent matches played with fellow members) dictates their bubble size and pulls them onto closer inner orbits. Their Fidelity (the share of their matches played inside this clearing versus other homelands) shapes their color and standing:

Herald Top Weight Frog Tier The central pillar holding the highest internal match weight within the clearing.
Partisan Core Denizen Fidelity ≥ 65% Dedicated members whose matches take place primarily within their homeland.
Squire Regular 50% – 64% Active contributors who split a notable portion of their tables with outside clearings.
Friend Free Roamer Fidelity < 50% Borderlanders attached to the clearing while playing frequently across the wider forest.
Unaligned Vagabond No Clearing Independent competitors not currently anchored to any persistent seasonal homeland.

In the top corner of a selected clearing, the Chronicle records its historical milestones: when it was Founded, when it Culminated at peak membership, and whether it was Dissolved or later Revived. Clicking any milestone rewinds the map directly to that day.

The Player’s Circle • Personal Networks

Double-clicking any player bubble—or typing a name into the search bar—switches the map to that competitor's Player’s Circle. This view sets aside global politics to focus on an individual's personal table companions across the entire season:

The more games you share with a companion, the larger their bubble grows and the closer they orbit around your center. Alongside the graph, the Allegiances ledger tracks every clearing you have belonged to during the season. Clicking any allegiance badge transports you back to that clearing on the exact day you joined its ranks.

The Louvain Compass • Community Detection

Under the hood, the Woodland is modeled as a dynamic mathematical graph where players are nodes and shared matches form weighted trails between them. Every day, the engine charts communities using the Louvain method—a modularity optimization algorithm designed to uncover natural clusters inside complex networks.

Rather than forcing fixed factions, the algorithm compares the density of games played inside a group against the games expected by pure chance across the league. When a circle of active competitors shares significantly more tables together than with the rest of the forest, a Homeland naturally crystallizes.

The Laws of Emergence • Gravity & Decay

To prevent the map from flickering erratically after every match while still reflecting shifting seasonal habits, the simulation enforces a set of organic physical rules:

Trail Decay −5% / day γ = 0.95 Every match adds 1.0 weight to a pair's bond, while existing bonds decay by 5% each active match day so recent fellowships outweigh old history.
Foundation Birth & Cap 6+ Players Up to 5 clearings can coexist. A new clearing requires at least 6 eligible active players with dense internal ties to claim a woodland totem.
Stickiness Inertia ×1.5 Pull A player only defects to a rival clearing if their pull toward the new group exceeds their current homeland bond by at least 50% (capped at 3 transfers/day).
Dissolution Survival Floor < 4 Players Players inactive for 21 days return to the unaligned wilds. If a clearing drops below 4 active denizens, it dissolves—though it can be Revived if 3 former members reunite.
