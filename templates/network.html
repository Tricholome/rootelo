The Homelands Map • Woodland Clearings

While the Leaderboard tracks individual skill through Elo ratings, Homelands maps the social fabric of the league. Rather than asking who defeats whom, this cartography reveals who shares the table with whom. Recurring playgroups naturally crystallize into Clearings rallied around an iconic woodland item, sized by their population of active denizens and linked by dashed trails. Use the bottom date controls to navigate day by day through the season and watch clearings emerge, grow, or fade away.

The Clearing View • Fidelity, Herald & Chronicles

Clicking a clearing isolates its internal constellation. Each member's Weight (volume of recent matches played with fellow members) determines their bubble size and pulls them onto closer inner orbits, crowning the top contributor as the clearing's Herald (Frog tier). Meanwhile, a player's Fidelity (the share of their games played inside this clearing versus other homelands) colors their bubble from Partisan (≥ 65%), through Squire (50%–64%), to Friend (< 50%). In the top-right corner, the Chronicle badges let you jump directly to key historical milestones (Founded, Culminated, Dissolved, or Revived).

The Player’s Circle • Companions & Allegiances

Clicking any player bubble—or searching a name in the top bar—switches the map to their personal Player’s Circle across the entire season. The more matches shared with a companion, the larger their bubble grows and the closer they orbit around the center. Bubble colors distinguish partners belonging to the same homeland, another homeland, or unaligned wanderers, while the Allegiances ledger on the right lets you jump back to every clearing the player has belonged to during the season.

The Louvain Compass • Community Detection

Under the hood, the Woodland is modeled as a dynamic mathematical graph where players are nodes and shared matches form weighted trails between them. Every active day, the engine charts communities using the Louvain method—a modularity optimization algorithm designed to uncover natural clusters inside complex networks.

Rather than forcing fixed factions, the algorithm compares the density of games played inside a group against the games expected by pure chance across the league. When a circle of active competitors shares significantly more tables together than with the rest of the forest, a Homeland naturally crystallizes.

The Laws of Emergence • Gravity & Decay

To prevent the map from flickering erratically after every match while still reflecting shifting seasonal habits, the simulation enforces four physical rules:

Trail Decay −5% / day γ = 0.95 Every match adds 1.0 weight to a pair's bond, while existing bonds decay by 5% each active match day so recent fellowships outweigh old history.
Foundation Birth & Cap 6+ Players Up to 5 clearings can coexist. A new clearing requires at least 6 eligible active players with dense internal ties to claim a woodland item.
Stickiness Inertia ×1.5 Pull A player only transfers to a rival clearing if their pull toward the new group exceeds their current homeland bond by at least 50% (max 3 transfers/day).
Dissolution Survival Floor < 4 Players Players inactive for 21 days return to the unaligned wilds. If a clearing drops below 4 active denizens, it dissolves—though it can be Revived if 3 former members reunite.
