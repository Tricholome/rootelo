An Alternative Ladder

Rootelo is a rating engine built for competitive Root leagues. Designed for the unique chaos of four-player asymmetric warfare, it provides a clear view of who truly rules the Woodland.

The Scoring Engine

To ensure fairness in a four-player format, this leaderboard uses a multi-player Elo model. The journey begins with a base rating of 1200 points. Unlike a simple win-count, the engine evaluates the “strength of schedule”: defeating high-ranked opponents boosts a rating significantly, while losing as the favorite results in a steeper drop. The Elo score is permanent, serving as a continuous record of performance over time.

The Dynamic Phases

Your score moves much faster when you first start playing. This speed is controlled by the K-Factor, whose formula varies depending on the competition: point gains and losses are larger in early matches so you can quickly find your place, then naturally smooth out as your match count grows to reflect your long-term consistency.
