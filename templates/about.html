An Alternative Ladder

Rootelo is an automated rating engine designed to evaluate player skill and strategic consistency across competitive Root leagues. Rather than relying on simple win counts, this platform provides a continuous, strength-of-schedule evaluation tailored specifically to the asymmetrical nature of four-player woodland warfare.

The Scoring Engine

To ensure fairness in a four-player format, this leaderboard uses a multi-player Elo model. The journey begins with a base rating of 1200 points. Unlike a simple win-count, the engine evaluates the “strength of schedule”: defeating high-ranked opponents boosts a rating significantly, while losing as the favorite results in a steeper drop. The Elo score is permanent, serving as a continuous record of performance over time.

The Dynamic K-Factor

The K-Factor determines the weight of a single match on a player's rating—essentially measuring rating volatility. A high K-factor allows rapid adjustments for new or unranked players, while a lower K-factor ensures long-term stability for established veterans. To avoid harsh artificial step brackets, Rootelo relies on a continuous exponential decay formula. Volatility starts high during your initial matches and smoothly decays toward a stable minimum floor as your match count increases.
